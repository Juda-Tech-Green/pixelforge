## Coordinate contract

Character and raster coordinates: +X right, +Y down, radians clockwise. A joint's
offset is in its parent's unrounded frame; its local rotation affects outgoing
bones/attachments. Root translation is applied once. Bone lengths come from rest
offset vectors and never from interpolated pixel positions. Joint-space angles
use shortest-arc interpolation, with explicit target/contact solving afterwards.

Scene coordinates: (x,y,z), X right, Y along ground depth, Z upward. Side view
projects (x,-z), top-down (x,y), three-quarter uses configurable 2x3 axes. Camera
translation/scale apply after projection. Inverse mapping requires a specified
plane and a nonzero 2x2 determinant; invisible depth is never guessed.

Only raster boundaries round, using floor(value+.5), including negative halves.
All graph traversal/work is bounded; cycles and unsatisfiable occlusion fail.
Validation diagnoses mathematical constraints separately from aesthetic quality.
